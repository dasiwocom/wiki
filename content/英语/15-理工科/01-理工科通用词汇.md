## 常说的句子

Let's assume...:假设……
Suppose that...:假定……
It follows that...:由此可得……
This is proportional to that.:这个和那个成正比。
As x increases, y decreases.:x 增大时 y 减小。
To a first approximation...:作一级近似……
Let's neglect the friction.:先忽略摩擦。
The error is within tolerance.:误差在公差范围内。
The result is consistent with theory.:结果和理论一致。
That's an order of magnitude larger.:大了整整一个数量级。
It holds for small values only.:只在小数值时成立。
Assume ideal conditions.:假设理想条件。
Round it to two decimals.:保留两位小数。
The units don't match.:单位对不上。
