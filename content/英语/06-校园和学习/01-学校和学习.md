## 校园里常用的话

What's your major?:你学什么专业？
I'm majoring in mechanical engineering.:我学机械工程。
I'm a sophomore.:我大二。
Which class are you in?:你在哪个班？
What time does the class start?:几点上课？
The class was cancelled.:课取消了。
The class was put off.:课推迟了。
Where's the lecture hall?:阶梯教室在哪？
I have two classes this afternoon.:我下午有两节课。
I missed the lecture.:我缺了那节课。
Can I borrow your notes?:能借下你的笔记吗？
Did you take notes?:你记笔记了吗？
I'll send you the slides.:我把课件发你。
Could you explain it again?:能再讲一遍吗？
I didn't catch that.:我没听清。
Let's study together.:一起学习吧。
I need to go to the library.:我得去图书馆。
I'm staying in the dorm tonight.:我今晚待宿舍。
What's the assignment?:作业是什么？
When is it due?:什么时候交？
