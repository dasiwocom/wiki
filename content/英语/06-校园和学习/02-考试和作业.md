## 小组讨论

Let's get started.:我们开始吧。
Who wants to go first?:谁先来？
What do you think?:你觉得呢？
That's a good idea.:这想法不错。
I agree with you.:我同意你。
I'm not sure about that.:这个我不太确定。
Can I add something?:我能补充一点吗？
Let me finish.:让我说完。
Sorry, go ahead.:抱歉，你说。
Could you explain that?:能解释一下吗？
What do you mean by that?:你这是什么意思？
Let's move on.:我们继续下一个。
We're running out of time.:时间快不够了。
Let's split the work.:我们分一下工。
I'll take this part.:这部分我来。
Can you handle that?:那部分你能搞定吗？
Let's meet again on Friday.:周五再碰一次吧。
I'll send the file tonight.:我今晚把文件发出去。

## 和老师交流

Excuse me, may I ask a question?:打扰一下，我能问个问题吗？
I have a question about...:我有个关于……的问题。
Could you go over that again?:能再讲一遍吗？
I don't quite follow.:我没太跟上。
Could you give me some feedback?:能给我点反馈吗？
I'd like to discuss my grade.:我想谈谈我的成绩。
Could I have an extension?:能延期吗？
I was sick last week.:我上周病了。
I couldn't make it to class.:我没能来上课。
Thank you for your time.:谢谢您抽时间。
I really appreciate your help.:非常感谢您的帮助。
I'll revise it and resubmit.:我改完再交一遍。
