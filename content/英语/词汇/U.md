## underline 强调
Underline any word you don't know while reading.阅读时把你不会的词划出来。
Please underline the key sentences in the passage.请划出文中的关键句子。
The report seeks to underline the urgency of climate action.报告意在强调气候行动的紧迫性。
The study's findings underline the significance of sustainable development in urban planning.研究结果强调了可持续发展在城市规划中的重要意义。

## undergo 经受
The company is undergoing big changes right now.公司目前正经历重大变革。
The building will undergo repair.这座建筑将接受修缮。
New products undergo strict safety tests before sale.新产品上市前要经受严格安全测试。
The ecosystem may undergo irreversible changes under continuous human disturbance.在持续的人类干扰下，生态系统可能发生不可逆变化。

---
## unacceptable 不能接受的
That behaviour is totally unacceptable, period.那种行为完全不能接受，就这样。
This mistake is unacceptable for a student.对学生来说这个错误是不能接受的。
The committee deemed the proposal unacceptable.委员会认为该提案无法接受。
It is unacceptable to compromise safety for the sake of profit.为了利润而牺牲安全是不能接受的。
