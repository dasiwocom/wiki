## kernel 内核
A kernel panic will force your server to shut down instantly.内核崩溃会强制服务器立即关机。
The kernel manages the memory and processes of the system.内核管理系统内存和进程。
This open-source kernel ensures compatibility with a wide range of peripheral hardware.这款开源内核确保与各类外设硬件兼容。
Modifying the kernel demands profound expertise in computer architecture and memory management.修改内核需要深厚的计算机架构与内存管理专业知识。
