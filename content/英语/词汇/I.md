## incorrect 错误的
That answer is incorrect, try again.那个答案是错的，再试一次。
Your answer to this question is incorrect.你对这道题的答案是错误的。
The hypothesis turned out to be incorrect after rigorous testing.经过严谨检验，该假设被证实是错误的。
It is incorrect to assume that economic growth automatically guarantees social equality.假定经济增长会自动保障社会公平是错误的。
