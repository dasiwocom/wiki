## nurse 护士
The nurse will be with you in a moment.护士一会儿就来。
The nurse takes care of patients.护士照料病人。
The experienced nurse handles emergencies efficiently.经验丰富的护士高效处理急症。
A professional nurse provides continuous support for hospitalised patients.专业护士为住院患者提供持续照料。

## navy 海军
His grandpa served in the navy for twenty years.他爷爷在海军服役二十年。
The navy protects the coast from attack.海军保卫海岸免受攻击。
The navy carried out a major military exercise.海军举行了大规模军事演习。
A strong navy has historically been crucial to national maritime security.强大的海军历来对国家海上安全至关重要。
