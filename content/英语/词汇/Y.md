## year 年
I haven't seen him in years.我好几年没见到他了。
We have an English exam once a year.我们每年举行一次英语考试。
The nation has witnessed rapid economic growth year after year.这个国家逐年见证快速的经济增长。
The species completes its life cycle within a single year under favourable conditions.在适宜条件下，该物种在一年内完成生命周期。
