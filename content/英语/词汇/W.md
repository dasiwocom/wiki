## witness 见证
I witnessed the whole accident with my own eyes.我亲眼目击了整场事故。
She was a witness of the car accident.她是这场车祸的目击者。
The ancient monument witnesses centuries of cultural transformation.这座古老遗迹见证数百年的文化变迁。
She was fortunate to witness the historic signing ceremony of the international treaty.她有幸见证这项国际条约的历史性签署仪式。
