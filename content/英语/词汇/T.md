## then 然后
I'll call you, and then we can go out.我打电话给你，然后我们出去。
First wash your hands, then have dinner.先洗手，然后吃饭。
The economy boomed, then suffered a sudden recession.经济一度繁荣，然后突然衰退。
First collect raw data, then analyse and interpret the results systematically.先收集原始数据，然后系统性地分析解读结果。
