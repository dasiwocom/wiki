## 常用句

I'd like a kilo of apples.:我要一公斤苹果。
Are these ripe?:这些熟了吗？
Can I have a taste?:我能尝一个吗？
How much per kilo?:多少钱一公斤？
Peel the skin off.:把皮削掉。
Cut it into slices.:切成片。
It's a bit sour.:有点酸。
These grapes are really sweet.:这葡萄特别甜。
I eat fruit every day.:我每天都吃水果。
Apples are my favorite.:苹果是我最喜欢的。
