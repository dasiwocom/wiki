# 06 技术沟通常用表达

## 描述问题

It doesn't work.:不行。
It's not working.:它不工作。
Something's wrong.:有点不对劲。
It keeps crashing.:一直崩。
It threw an error.:报错了。
I got an error message.:我这儿弹了个报错。
It's stuck.:卡住了。
It's frozen.:死机了。
It won't load.:加载不出来。
Nothing happens.:什么都没发生。
It works on my machine.:我这边能跑。
It used to work.:以前是好的。
It broke after the update.:更新之后就坏了。
I can't get it to run.:我跑不起来。
It's not doing what I expected.:结果跟我想的不一样。
It only happens sometimes.:只是偶尔出现。
It happens every time.:每次都会。
I can reproduce it.:我能复现。
I can't reproduce it.:我复现不出来。

## 求助

Can you help me with this?:能帮我看看这个吗？
I'm stuck on this.:我卡在这一步了。
Could you take a look?:你能看一下吗？
How do I do this?:这个怎么做？
What does this mean?:这是什么意思？
What's the difference between A and B?:A 和 B 有什么区别？
Which one should I use?:我该用哪个？
Am I doing this right?:我这么做对吗？
Is this the right way to do it?:这是正确的做法吗？
Can you explain it in simpler terms?:能用简单点的话说吗？
Can you give me an example?:能给我个例子吗？
Can you walk me through it?:能带我走一遍吗？
What am I missing?:我漏了什么？
Where should I start?:我该从哪儿开始？
Do you have a minute?:你有空吗？

## 澄清和确认

Could you clarify that?:能说清楚点吗？
What do you mean by that?:你这话什么意思？
Can you be more specific?:能具体一点吗？
Let me make sure I understand.:我确认一下我理解得对不对。
So you're saying that...?:所以你的意思是……？
Correct me if I'm wrong.:我说错了你纠正我。
In other words...:换句话说……
Just to be clear...:先说清楚……
Does that make sense?:这样说得通吗？
Am I following you?:我跟上了吗？
Let me rephrase that.:我换个说法。
I mean...:我的意思是……
To put it another way...:换个角度说……

## 说自己不确定

I'm not sure.:我不确定。
I have no idea.:我完全不知道。
I'm not familiar with that.:这个我不熟。
I've never used it before.:我没用过。
I think so, but I'm not certain.:我觉得是，但不确定。
It might be...:可能是……
As far as I know...:据我所知……
I could be wrong.:我可能说错。
Don't quote me on that.:别把这话当准的。
Roughly speaking...:大致来说……
Off the top of my head...:凭印象说……
It depends.:看情况。
That depends on...:那得看……

## 说进展

I'm working on it.:我在弄了。
I'm still figuring it out.:还在摸索。
I've almost got it.:快好了。
I got it working.:我把它跑起来了。
I fixed it.:修好了。
I figured it out.:我搞明白了。
It's done.:完成了。
I'm halfway there.:做了一半。
I'm running into a problem.:我碰到个问题。
I'm stuck at step 3.:我卡在第 3 步。
It's taking longer than expected.:比预想的慢。
I'll get back to you.:我回头再回你。
Give me a few minutes.:给我几分钟。

## 给反馈

That's helpful.:这个有用。
That worked.:成了。
That's exactly what I needed.:正是我要的。
That didn't work.:没用。
Not quite.:还差一点。
Close, but not quite.:接近了，但还不对。
That's not what I meant.:我不是这个意思。
It's better now.:现在好多了。
The result is off.:结果不对。
That's way off.:差得远。
Good enough.:够用了。
Perfect.:完美。
I'll take it.:就这个了。

## 让对方调整说法

Can you rephrase that?:能换个说法吗？
Can you say that again more slowly?:能慢点再说一遍吗？
Can you repeat that?:能重复一下吗？
Can you break it down?:能拆开讲吗？
Can you simplify it?:能简化一下吗？
Give me the short version.:说简短点。
Give me more detail.:说详细点。
Go on.:继续。
Keep going.:接着说。
What do you mean?:你什么意思？
Sorry, I didn't catch that.:抱歉，我没听清。
How do you spell that?:那个怎么拼？
Can you write it down?:能写下来吗？

## 推演和讲道理

Let's assume...:假设……
For example...:比如说……
Suppose that...:假定……
On the other hand...:另一方面……
In that case...:那样的话……
That depends on...:那取决于……
More or less.:差不多。
Basically...:基本上……
Specifically...:具体来说……
In general...:一般来说……
In practice...:实际上……
In theory...:理论上……
As a result...:结果是……
That's why...:这就是为什么……
The point is...:重点是……
The problem is...:问题是……
There's a catch.:有个坑。
It's a trade-off.:这是取舍。
It comes down to...:归根结底是……

## 顺序和步骤

first:首先
then:然后
next:接着
after that:之后
finally:最后
meanwhile:与此同时
at the same time:同时
before that:在那之前
step by step:一步一步
one at a time:一次一个
in order:按顺序
in advance:提前
as soon as possible:尽快
the first step:第一步
the last step:最后一步

a little:稍微
a lot:很多
slightly:略微
significantly:明显
roughly:大概
exactly:正好
approximately:大约
at least:至少
at most:最多
more or less:差不多
almost:几乎
barely:勉强
in most cases:大多数情况下
once in a while:偶尔

## 礼貌和语气

I'd appreciate it.:我会很感激。
Thanks a lot.:多谢。
No worries.:没事。
My bad.:我的错。
Sorry about that.:抱歉。
That makes sense.:有道理。
Good point.:说得对。
Fair enough.:有道理、行吧。
No problem.:没问题。
Anytime.:随时。
You're welcome.:不客气。
Let me know.:告诉我一声。
I'll let you know.:我会通知你。
Keep me posted.:有消息告诉我。

## 收个尾

Let's move on.:继续下一个。
Let's wrap up.:收个尾吧。
To sum up...:总结一下……
All in all...:总的来说……
That's about it.:差不多就这些。
Anything else?:还有别的吗？
I think that covers it.:我觉得齐了。
Let's call it a day.:今天就到这儿吧。
Good talk.:聊得不错。
