## 问路

Excuse me, how do I get to...?:请问去……怎么走？
Where is the nearest subway station?:最近的地铁站在哪？
Is it far from here?:离这儿远吗？
How long does it take?:要多久？
Can I walk there?:我能走过去吗？
Is this the right way?:这条路对吗？
Which bus goes there?:哪路公交到那儿？
Where should I get off?:我该在哪一站下？
Turn left.:左转。
Turn right.:右转。
Go straight.:直走。
It's on your left.:在你左手边。
It's around the corner.:就在拐角。
It's across the street.:在马路对面。
It's next to the bank.:在银行旁边。
You can't miss it.:你不会错过的。
I'm lost.:我迷路了。
Could you show me on the map?:能在地图上指给我看吗？
