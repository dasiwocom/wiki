## 时间怎么说

What time is it?:几点了？
It's three o'clock.:三点。
It's half past three.:三点半。
It's a quarter past three.:三点一刻。
It's a quarter to four.:差一刻四点。
It's ten to four.:差十分四点。
It's 7 a.m.:早上七点。
It's 9 p.m.:晚上九点。
What's today's date?:今天几号？
It's October 3rd.:十月三号。
What day is it today?:今天星期几？
It's Monday.:星期一。
When is your birthday?:你生日什么时候？
How long does it take?:要多久？
It takes about an hour.:大约一小时。
It's been a long time.:已经很久了。
I'm running late.:我要迟到了。
I'll be there in five minutes.:我五分钟到。
Let's do it another time.:换个时间吧。
See you at half past six.:六点半见。
