# 02 购物和付钱

store:商店（美式）
supermarket:超市
mall:购物中心
market:市场
convenience store:便利店
department store:百货商店
pharmacy:药店
bookshop:书店
online shopping:网购
shopping cart:购物车
basket:购物篮
shelf:货架
aisle:货架通道
cashier:收银员
counter:柜台
customer:顾客
shop assistant:店员
receipt:收据

sample:样品

discount:折扣

## 问价和砍价

How much is it?:多少钱？
How much does this cost?:这个多少钱？
What's the price?:价格是多少？
That's too expensive.:太贵了。
It's a bit pricey.:有点贵。
Can you make it cheaper?:能便宜点吗？
Is there any discount?:有折扣吗？
Can I get a better price?:能再便宜点吗？
Do you have anything cheaper?:有更便宜的吗？
What's the lowest you can go?:最低多少？
That's a good deal.:挺划算的。
It's a bargain.:真便宜。
Money-back guarantee?:不满意能退吗？

## 付钱

pay:付款
cash:现金
card:卡
credit card:信用卡
debit card:借记卡
mobile payment:手机支付
change:零钱、找零
exact change:刚好够的零钱
Keep the change.:不用找了。
Do you take cards?:能刷卡吗？
Can I pay by phone?:能手机支付吗？
Could I have a receipt?:能给我张收据吗？
Is tax included?:含税吗？
I'll pay in cash.:我付现金。
Here's your change.:找您的钱。
That'll be 20 yuan.:一共 20 块。
Anything else?:还要别的吗？
No, that's all.:不用了，就这些。

## 退换货

I'd like to return this.:我想退这个。
I want to exchange this.:我想换这个。
Can I get a refund?:能退款吗？
Do you have the receipt?:有小票吗？
It doesn't fit.:尺寸不合适。
It's defective.:有质量问题。
It arrived damaged.:到货时是坏的。
Can I have a replacement?:能换个新的吗？
It's still under warranty.:还在保修期内。
Is it refundable?:能退款吗？
I bought it yesterday.:我昨天买的。
I changed my mind.:我改主意了。

I'm just browsing.:我随便看看。
I'll take it.:我要了。
Could you wrap it up?:能帮我包起来吗？
Can I get a bag?:能给个袋子吗？
I paid by card.:我刷的卡。
