## 酒店和旅行常用句

I have a reservation.:我有预订。
I'd like to book a room.:我想订间房。
Do you have any rooms available?:还有空房吗？
How much per night?:一晚多少钱？
Is breakfast included?:含早餐吗？
Can I see the room?:我能看看房间吗？
Could I have a wake-up call at seven?:能七点叫我起床吗？
The air conditioning isn't working.:空调坏了。
Could I have some more towels?:能再给我几条毛巾吗？
Can I leave my luggage here?:我能把行李寄在这儿吗？
I'd like to check out.:我要退房。
Can I pay by card?:能刷卡吗？
What's the checkout time?:几点退房？
Could you call a taxi for me?:能帮我叫辆车吗？
Is there a bus to the airport?:有机场大巴吗？
Where can I buy tickets?:在哪买票？
How much is the entrance fee?:门票多少钱？
Are there any student discounts?:有学生优惠吗？
Could you take a photo for me?:能帮我拍张照吗？
What do you recommend?:你推荐去哪儿？
Do you have a map?:有地图吗？
When does it open?:几点开门？
It was a wonderful trip.:这趟旅行太棒了。
