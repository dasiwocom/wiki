## old 旧的
This old jacket has seen better days.这件旧夹克已经破旧不堪。
This is an old house.这是一栋老房子。
The old town preserves many traditional buildings.这座旧城镇保留了许多传统建筑。
Old cultural relics offer direct evidence of ancient civilisation.旧文物提供古文明的直接证据。

## outbreak 爆发
There was an outbreak of flu at the school.学校爆发了流感。
The outbreak of the disease worried people.疾病爆发让人们担忧。
The sudden outbreak of war changed everything.战争的突然爆发改变了一切。
The outbreak highlighted weaknesses in the public health system.这场爆发暴露了公共卫生体系的弱点。

## opposing 对立的
We're on opposing sides of this argument, and neither will back down.在这场争论中我们站在对立两边，谁也不肯让步。
The two teams have opposing views on the plan.两支队伍对这个计划持对立观点。
Opposing factions failed to reach a compromise.对立派别未能达成妥协。
Debate thrives when opposing perspectives are presented with sound evidence.当对立观点配以充分证据时，辩论才会蓬勃发展。
