# 03 家务和作息

## 一天的作息

wake up:醒来
get up:起床
get out of bed:下床
oversleep:睡过头
sleep in:睡懒觉
stay up late:熬夜
go to bed:上床睡觉
fall asleep:睡着
sleep:睡觉
rest:休息
take a nap:打个盹
have a lie-in:赖床（英式）
take a shower:洗淋浴
take a bath:泡澡
brush my teeth:刷牙
wash my face:洗脸
get dressed:穿衣服
get ready:做准备
have breakfast:吃早饭
head out:出门
come back home:回家
do homework:写作业
watch TV:看电视
scroll on my phone:刷手机
set an alarm:定闹钟
hit the snooze button:按掉闹钟再来一会儿

## 家务

do the housework:做家务
clean up:收拾整理
tidy up:拾掇干净
sweep the floor:扫地
mop the floor:拖地
vacuum:用吸尘器吸
wipe the table:擦桌子
dust:掸灰
do the dishes:洗碗
wash the dishes:洗碗
dry the dishes:擦干碗
load the dishwasher:把碗放进洗碗机
take out the trash:倒垃圾
do the laundry:洗衣服
hang up the laundry:晾衣服
fold the clothes:叠衣服
iron the clothes:熨衣服
make the bed:铺床
change the sheets:换床单
water the plants:浇花
walk the dog:遛狗
feed the cat:喂猫
clean the bathroom:打扫卫生间
cook dinner:做晚饭
set the table:摆碗筷
share the chores:分担家务
a chore:一件家务活

## 和室友相处

roommate:室友
flatmate:合租室友（英式）
landlord:房东
house rules:家规
quiet hours:安静时段
mess:乱、脏乱
messy:乱糟糟的
tidy:整洁的
it's your turn:轮到你了
my turn:该我了
Do you mind if I...?:你介意我……吗？
Would you mind keeping it down?:能小声点吗？
Could you clean up after yourself?:你能顺手收拾一下吗？
I'll get it next time.:下次我来。
Sorry, I'll do it now.:抱歉，我马上做。
Let's split the chores.:我们分一下家务吧。
I'm on duty today.:今天我值日。

## 常用句

I overslept and missed breakfast.:我睡过头了，没吃上早饭。
I stayed up too late last night.:我昨晚熬夜太晚了。
I'm exhausted.:我累坏了。
I still have laundry to do.:我还有衣服要洗。
The laundry is still wet.:衣服还没干。
Can you do the dishes tonight?:今晚你洗碗行吗？
I'll take out the trash.:我去倒垃圾。
The kitchen is a mess.:厨房乱得不行。
Let's clean up together.:我们一起收拾吧。
I need to get some sleep.:我得去睡觉了。
