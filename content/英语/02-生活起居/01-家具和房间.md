## 租房子看房子

apartment / flat:公寓
house:房子
landlord:房东
tenant:租客
rent:租金
deposit:押金
utility bill:水电费
furnished:带家具的
unfurnished:不带家具的
upstairs:楼上
downstairs:楼下
ground floor:一楼
lift / elevator:电梯
square meter:平方米
neighborhood:小区、街区
How much is the rent?:房租多少？
Is it furnished?:带家具吗？
Are bills included?:包水电吗？
When can I move in?:我什么时候能搬进来？
I'm looking for a room.:我在找房子。
Can I take a look?:我能看看吗？

## 常用句

Make yourself at home.:别客气，当自己家。
Your room is upstairs.:你的房间在楼上。
The key is on the table.:钥匙在桌上。
Lock the door when you leave.:走的时候锁门。
The light isn't working.:灯不亮了。
The tap is leaking.:水龙头在漏水。
It's on the second floor.:在二楼。
There's a lift around the corner.:拐角有电梯。
