## 看病时说的话

I'd like to make an appointment.:我想预约。
What's wrong?:哪里不舒服？
What seems to be the problem?:怎么了？
How long have you had this?:这样多久了？
Does it hurt here?:这儿疼吗？
Open your mouth, please.:请张嘴。
Take a deep breath.:深呼吸。
I'll prescribe you some medicine.:我给你开点药。
Take it twice a day.:一天吃两次。
Take it after meals.:饭后吃。
Get some rest.:多休息。
Drink plenty of water.:多喝水。
You'll be fine.:你会没事的。
Come back in a week.:一周后再来。
Where does it hurt?:哪儿疼？
I'm allergic to penicillin.:我对青霉素过敏。
Do I need a shot?:我需要打针吗？

## 常用句

I'm trying to lose weight.:我在试着减肥。
Let's go for a run.:去跑步吧。
I pulled a muscle.:我拉伤了肌肉。
I twisted my ankle.:我扭了脚踝。
Do you play any sports?:你玩什么运动？
