## jump 跳跃
Don't jump on the bed!别在床上跳！
The kids jump happily in the yard.孩子们在院子里开心地跳。
The figures jump sharply after the policy was implemented.政策实施后，该数值急剧上升。
House prices may jump dramatically due to the limited housing supply.由于住房供给不足，房价可能大幅上涨。
