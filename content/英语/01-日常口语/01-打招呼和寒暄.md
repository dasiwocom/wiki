# 01 打招呼和寒暄

## 见面打招呼

Hi! / Hey!:嗨！
Hello there.:你好呀。
Good morning.:早上好。
Good afternoon.:下午好。
Good evening.:晚上好。
How are you?:你好吗？
How's it going?:最近怎么样？
How are you doing?:你最近怎么样？
How have you been?:这一阵过得怎么样？
What's up?:最近咋样？（很随意）
Long time no see.:好久不见。
It's been a while.:有阵子没见了。
Nice to see you again.:又见到你真好。
Fancy seeing you here.:真巧，在这儿碰上你。
Good to see you.:见到你真好。

## 回答「你好吗」

I'm good, thanks. And you?:我挺好的，谢谢。你呢？
Pretty good.:挺不错的。
Not bad.:还行。
Can't complain.:没什么可抱怨的。
So-so.:一般般。
Could be better.:还能更好吧。
Keeping busy.:一直挺忙的。
Same as usual.:老样子。
Hanging in there.:还撑得住。

## 第一次见面

Nice to meet you.:很高兴认识你。
Nice to meet you too.:我也很高兴认识你。
I'm Ming. / My name is Ming.:我叫小明。
What's your name?:你叫什么名字？
May I ask your name?:请问您贵姓？
How do you spell that?:怎么拼？
Where are you from?:你是哪里人？
I'm from Shandong.:我是山东人。
What do you do?:你是做什么的？
I'm a student.:我是学生。
This is my friend, Li.:这是我朋友小李。
Have we met before?:我们见过吗？
I've heard a lot about you.:久仰久仰。
Pleasure to meet you.:幸会。

## 寒暄找话题

How's your day going?:今天过得怎么样？
How was your weekend?:周末过得怎么样？
Any plans for the weekend?:周末有什么安排？
How's the family?:家里都好吗？
How's work?:工作怎么样？
How's school?:学校怎么样？
What have you been up to?:最近在忙什么？
How's the weather today?:今天天气怎么样？
Nice weather, isn't it?:天气不错啊。
Busy day?:今天很忙？
Are you here on vacation?:你是来度假的吗？
Have you eaten yet?:吃了吗？（中式寒暄）

## 告别

Bye. / Bye-bye.:再见。
See you.:回头见。
See you later.:待会儿见。
See you tomorrow.:明天见。
See you around.:有空再见。
Good night.:晚安。
Take care.:保重。
Have a good day.:今天愉快。
Have a nice weekend.:周末愉快。
Have a safe trip.:一路平安。
Take it easy.:别太累，放松点。
Catch you later.:回头聊。
Talk to you later.:待会儿聊。
I'd better get going.:我得走了。
I have to run.:我得赶紧走了。
It was nice talking to you.:跟你聊得很开心。
Let's keep in touch.:保持联系。
Say hi to your family for me.:替我向你家人问好。

## 常用寒暄句

How's everything?:一切都好吧？
How's it been?:最近怎么样？
What's new?:有什么新鲜事？
Nothing much.:没什么特别的。
Not much, you?:没什么，你呢？
Same old, same old.:还是老样子。
Things are looking up.:情况在好转。
I've been swamped.:我忙得不可开交。
I've been under the weather.:我身体有点不舒服。
Give me a call sometime.:有空给我打电话。
Let's grab coffee sometime.:找时间一起喝杯咖啡。
We should hang out more.:我们该多聚聚。

## 见面小对话

Hey, long time no see! How have you been?:嗨，好久不见！最近怎么样？
Pretty good, thanks. How about you?:挺好的，谢谢。你呢？
Not bad. Work's been crazy lately.:还行。最近工作忙疯了。
Tell me about it.:可不是嘛。
Anyway, I should get going. See you around!:那我先走了，有空再见！
Take care! Let's keep in touch.:保重！保持联系。
It was great seeing you.:见到你真好。
Say hello to your parents for me.:替我问候你爸妈。
