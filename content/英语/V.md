## variable 变量
You forgot to reset this variable before the loop.循环开始前你忘了重置这个变量。
This variable stores the counter value.这个变量存储计数值。
Meaningful variable names greatly improve code readability.有意义的变量名能大幅提升代码可读性。
Global variables can introduce unintended coupling between distinct modules.全局变量可能在不同模块间引入非预期的耦合。

## variable 可变的
The weather's so variable here, you never know what to wear.这里天气多变，你永远不知道该穿什么。
The temperature is variable in autumn.秋天温度多变。
Variable costs change with the level of production.可变成本随生产水平变化。
Multiple variable factors influence the outcome of the experiment.多种可变因素影响实验的结果。

## voluntary 自愿的
She does voluntary work at the animal shelter.她在动物收容所做志愿工作。
He made a voluntary donation.他做了自愿捐款。
Participation in the programme is entirely voluntary.参与该计划完全是自愿的。
Voluntary service contributes significantly to community welfare.志愿服务对社区福利贡献巨大。
