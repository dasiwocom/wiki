## 常说的句子

Set up the machine.:调设备、装夹。
The part is out of tolerance.:零件超差了。
Tighten the bolt.:把螺栓拧紧。
Run a test.:跑个测试。
The line is down.:产线停了。
It's back online.:恢复了。
We need to hit the target.:得达到目标产量。
Check the drawing.:对一下图纸。
Which process comes first?:哪道工序在前？
That's within spec.:在规格范围内。
It failed inspection.:检验没通过。
Schedule a maintenance window.:排个停机维护时间。
The sensor reading is off.:传感器读数不对。
Upload the program to the chip.:把程序烧进芯片。
It won't respond.:没反应。
