## pin 引脚
Double-check which pin you plugged the wire into.再确认一下你把线插到了哪个引脚。
Connect the LED to pin number one.把LED连接到一号引脚。
Setting the pin to a high level activates the connected device.将该引脚置为高电平会激活连接的设备。
Each microcontroller pin serves a dedicated function defined by the datasheet.每个单片机引脚都承担数据手册所定义的特定功能。

## part-time 兼职的
I work part-time at a coffee shop on weekends.我周末在一家咖啡店做兼职。
He has a part-time job at the library.他在图书馆有一份兼职。
Many overseas students seek part-time employment to cover living costs.很多留学生找兼职工作补贴生活开支。
Part-time workers often face less social welfare protection than their full-time counterparts.兼职从业者往往比全职人员面临更少的社会保障。
